Cozy Orbit plays sound through OpenAL Soft, used unmodified as a dynamically loaded library
(Windows: soft_oal.dll, macOS: libopenal.dylib — taken from the Silk.NET.OpenAL.Soft.Native 1.23.1 NuGet package).

Replacing the library: put your own build named soft_oal.dll (Windows) or libopenal.dylib (macOS)
next to the CozyOrbit executable (macOS: Cozy Orbit.app/Contents/MacOS/). The game loads that file
first, before its bundled copy.
